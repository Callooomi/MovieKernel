How to play

Tenable

Fill in as many correct answers as you can for the given category. Type a guess (suggestions appear as you type); correct answers fill the board. Find them all before your three lives run out.

Bingo

Match the named actor to a square that fits them to mark it off. Complete the whole board to win. An incorrect guess skips you forward two actors; use Skip if you're stuck.

Higher or Lower

Two actors are shown side by side with one stat — age, box office, leading roles, or Oscar nominations. Guess whether the hidden actor's stat is higher or lower. Keep going for as long as you can; one wrong guess ends the run.

Networks

Five columns of tiles hide six connecting paths. Pick one tile per column to trace a valid path and submit it; correct paths lock in. You have three lives — find as many of the six as you can.
